Overview
------------------------------
The EU MFA is integrated into MIC3, the open modelling framework developed within TRANSIENCE to assess industrial
circularity and decarbonisation. Within the modelling chain, the EU MFA connects socioeconomic and energy-system
developments with physical material stocks, flows, demand, recycling and production requirements.


Inputs from other modules
------------------------------

The EU MFA receives socioeconomic drivers from OPEN-GEM, vehicle deployment
pathways from OPEN-PROM and product-level assumptions from the Product and
Service Database. In addition, REMIND-MFA returns modelled imports and exports
to embed European material flows in a global, mass-balanced trade system.

Outputs to other modules
------------------------------

The EU MFA provides physical material-flow results to REMIND-MFA and the
industrial models. These outputs connect material demand and circularity
strategies with trade, production pathways, technology deployment, energy use
and production costs.

Harmonisation
------------------------------
The participating modules differ in their sectoral, product, technological and
regional resolution. Dedicated correspondence tables and conversion scripts
therefore aggregate or disaggregate the exchanged data and align classifications,
units, geographical coverage and modelling periods.
